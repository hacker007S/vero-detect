# VeRO Detect — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 20 August 2026
**Contact:** zahoor@pycode.co.uk (Pycode Ltd, United Kingdom)

## What VeRO Detect does
VeRO Detect is a browser extension that checks product listings you view on
eBay, Amazon and AliExpress against eBay's VeRO programme and UK
restricted-item rules, to help sellers avoid policy violations. This is its
single purpose.

## Data we collect
**None.** VeRO Detect has no server. We do not collect, transmit, store,
sell or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage data.
There are no analytics, no tracking, and no accounts.

## How the extension processes data
- **Website content (processed locally):** when you open a product page on a
  supported site, the extension reads that page's title and details **inside
  your browser** to run its rule checks. This content never leaves your
  device, except in the one optional case below.
- **Optional AI deep check (user-initiated):** if you choose to add your own
  AI API key (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic) in the extension's
  settings and click "Deep check", the current listing's text is sent
  directly from your browser to that AI provider using **your** key, under
  that provider's privacy policy. This never happens automatically. Your API
  keys are stored only in your browser's local extension storage and are
  never sent anywhere except to the provider you chose.
- **Rules updates:** the extension periodically downloads its rules data
  (a public JSON file of brand names and restricted-item terms) from GitHub.
  This request sends no personal data and contains no executable code.

## Data sharing
We share no data with anyone, because we hold no data. We do not sell data,
do not use data for advertising, and do not transfer data to data brokers.

## Your choices
Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored settings. To have any
question answered, email zahoor@pycode.co.uk.
