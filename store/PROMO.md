## 1. Full message (WhatsApp broadcast to members)

*VeRO Detect is now live on the Chrome Web Store* 🎉

If you sell on eBay you already know the risk. One VeRO complaint and the listing is gone, and if it happens a few times the account goes with it. The painful part is that it is usually a brand you had never even heard of, quietly enrolled in eBay's VeRO programme, and you only find out after the removal email lands.

VeRO Detect tells you before you list, not after.

*How it works*

Install it once, then open any product on eBay, Amazon or AliExpress the way you normally would. A small badge appears in the bottom right corner of the page and gives you a straight verdict:

✅ CLEAR, no known flags
⚠️ CAUTION, there are conditions to sort out first
❌ DO NOT LIST, something is blocking it
❓ CHECK MANUALLY, the page did not give enough detail

*What it checks on every product*

🛡️ VeRO brands, over 1,100 of them, taken from eBay's own list and refreshed automatically every week
🚫 UK prohibited and restricted items across 7 categories
🏷️ Brand and trademark mentions in the title, including the misspellings sellers use to try to slip through
📦 Size and weight against the large letter limit, so you know your real postage cost before you buy the stock
⚠️ Sensitive classes such as skincare claims, toys and UKCA marking, batteries and food
🥂 Fragile items that will quietly eat your profit in returns
🚚 Dropship fit, including the cost gate and the AliExpress Choice packaging problem

There is also an optional AI deep check that reads the listing images and looks for logos you might have missed. That one is switched off by default and only runs if you add your own free API key.

*Your data stays yours*

Everything runs inside your browser. There is no server, no account and no sign up, and it collects nothing about you or the products you are researching.

*Install it here*
https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/kindbjoneleeodgpkfkgpileajllhbom

Click Add to Chrome, then open any product page. The badge appears on its own, there is nothing to set up.

*One honest thing*

It checks known rules only. A green result means nothing we know about is blocking you, it is not a promise from eBay. Treat it as your first filter and keep your own judgement on top of it.
